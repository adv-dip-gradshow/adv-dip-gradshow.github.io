Enter the Next Phase, an exhibition where the storytellers of tomorrow ready themselves to shape the creative worlds of today.

The Animation and Game Art students graduating from North Metropolitan TAFE are raring to leap into the industry, and we want you to join in the celebration of this exciting moment.

Step into a fantastical blend of visual mediums as we guide you through the transformation of sprawling labyrinths of intricate concept artwork into breathtaking vistas, gallant heroes, snarling horrors, and fascinating automata that dance before your eyes. Peek behind the curtain and witness the meticulous craftsmanship involved in optimising striking 3D sculptures and refining gorgeous hand-panted animations, all ready for commercial productions.

This exhibition marks the moment the students are ready to take the creative industry by storm, and we want you to join us as we enter the Next Phase.
